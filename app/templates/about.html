About CheckMyLink UK

CheckMyLink UK is a free, independent, non-profit tool built to help UK residents quickly sense-check a link, phone number, or message they're unsure about. We are not a bank, a government body, the police, or a paid service - and we will never ask you for your personal or financial details.

What we're not affiliated with

CheckMyLink UK is not officially affiliated with Take Five to Stop Fraud, Action Fraud, any bank, or any government department. We built this tool to complement their advice, using free, publicly available data sources - see how it works.

Our limitations - please read this

  • We check against known scam and malware lists. A brand-new scam that hasn't been reported or listed anywhere yet may come back as "Safe" even if it isn't.
  • Our phone number check uses a small, illustrative list of publicly-reported scam number patterns. It is not exhaustive, and scam numbers change constantly.
  • A "Safe" result means we found no known warning signs - it is not a guarantee. Always use your own judgement.
  • If a message pressures you to act urgently, or asks for money, passwords, or personal details, treat it with suspicion regardless of what any tool says.

Take Five to Stop Fraud

This tool is built to complement, not replace, the UK's official Take Five to Stop Fraud advice: Stop · Challenge · Protect. Learn more at takefive-stopfraud.org.uk.

Think you've been scammed?

Report it for free to Action Fraud, the UK's national fraud reporting service: actionfraud.police.uk.
