Changelog

A simple, plain-English record of what's been added or fixed on CheckMyLink UK so far.

28 August 2026

Major expansion: message scanning, phone checks, and more.

  • You can now paste a whole message (not just a link) - we pull out any links and phone numbers inside it automatically.
  • New: UK phone number checking against a small list of known scam call patterns.
  • New: detection of common scam pressure phrases, like "act now" or "your account has been suspended".
  • The list of UK brands we check for impersonation has grown from 11 to over 30 - including more banks, PayPal, Amazon UK, utility providers, NHS, DVSA, insurers, and delivery firms.
  • New: shortened links (like bit.ly) are now followed to their real destination before being checked.
  • New: links that use a raw numeric address instead of a proper website name are now flagged.
  • New pages: How it works, About, Changelog, and Feedback.
28 August 2026

Fixed a gap in our brand-impersonation check: web addresses that contained a real brand's name inside an unrelated address (e.g. "barclays-secure-login.com") were not always being caught. They are now correctly flagged.

27 August 2026

Set up automated testing so every change is checked before it goes live.

27 August 2026

Initial launch. CheckMyLink UK went live with our core link checker: malware and phishing list checks, domain age checks, brand impersonation detection, and an SSL certificate check - all combined into one plain-English Safe / Caution / Danger result.
